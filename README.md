Lunaris is a compact Roblox companion for managing FFlags, saving profiles,
and switching between your favorite launchers. Apply your selected flags,
review each result, and keep everything organized in one place.

## Installation

Have Roblox and your preferred launcher installed first. Lunaris opens your
existing installation; it does not download or replace Roblox.

### Windows Setup

1. Open the [download page](https://github.com/lIIusionator/Lunaris-Client/releases/latest).
2. Download **`Lunaris-0.16.3-Setup.exe`** and run it.
3. Follow the setup steps, then open **Lunaris** from the Start menu.

### Portable version

1. Download **`Lunaris-0.16.3-Windows-x64.zip`** from the same page.
2. Extract the ZIP to a folder of your choice.
3. Open **`Lunaris.exe`** from the extracted folder.

## Features

- **Profiles and presets** — import or paste flags, edit values, save profiles,
  and browse built-in or community presets. Stack imports to merge new flags
  into your current list.
- **Launcher switching** — choose Fishstrap, Bloxstrap, Froststrap, the normal
  Roblox client, or a custom launcher. Launch a specific place with a Place ID.
- **Live flag controls** — choose **Singleton** or **Offsets** in Settings,
  apply selected flags, stop a batch, or undo eligible changes.
- **Detailed logs** — see every flag's result and the final totals in a clean
  compact or expanded preview. Scroll with the mouse wheel or visible draggable
  scrollbar, use Page Up / Page Down or Ctrl+Home / Ctrl+End, select text, copy
  everything, or export the log.
- **Compact interface** — a custom title bar, rounded buttons, hover animations,
  and a waiting indicator. A reduced-motion option is available.
- **In-app updates** — check for new releases and restart to install when ready.
- **Settings** — manage preferences and open **Credits** or **License**.

## Getting started

1. Open **Roblox** in Lunaris and select your installed launcher.
2. In **Injector**, import or paste flags, or load a preset from **Community**.
   Review the names, values, and enabled entries.
3. Choose **Play Roblox**, then **Check Roblox** once the client is open.
4. Choose your method in **Settings → Live method**, then return to Injector
   and select **Apply live**.
5. Open **Logs** to review the results. Use **Stop** to cancel a batch or
   **Undo live** to restore eligible changes from the current session.

Use **Save for launch** when you want to save flags for a later launch instead.
Unsupported flags may be skipped, and Roblox may ignore some saved settings.
Not every flag is available in every Roblox version.

## Updates

Open **Settings → Check now** to look for an update. You can also enable
**Check for updates when Lunaris opens**.

When an update is ready, choose **Settings → Restart & update** after your
current operation finishes. Saved profiles and settings are kept. Restarting
ends the current session's live Undo history.
